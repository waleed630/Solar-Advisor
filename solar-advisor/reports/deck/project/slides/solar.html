Solar in Lahore, measured

1,150

units a year from 1 kW of panels, measured on a real 10 kW home. January makes only 35% of May (smog, short days).

Common online estimates say 1,300 to 1,500 units per kW. Real data is lower: smog, dust and load shedding. November and December are still estimates. Monthly output follows NASA sunlight with a correlation of 0.94.
